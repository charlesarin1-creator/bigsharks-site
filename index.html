A Structured Gate for Business Evaluation and SME Discipline

BIG SHARKS LTD is a governance-first system designed to evaluate, document, and structure access to carefully selected Nigerian SMEs through transparent reporting and controlled evaluation stages.

System Summary

Operating Doctrine

The principles that define how the system behaves.

1. Verification over speed

3. Discipline over volume
