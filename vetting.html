Vetting Process

All participants and opportunities undergo structured review before approval or inclusion in the system.

1. Identity & Background Review

Basic identity confirmation and background information are assessed for clarity and authenticity.

2. Operational Assessment

Business structure, activity model, and operational consistency are reviewed for alignment with system standards.

3. Risk Evaluation

Potential operational, financial, and structural risks are documented and analyzed before approval decisions.

4. Compliance Screening

Ensures that all entries meet internal governance requirements and do not violate system policies.

5. Final Selection

Only a limited number of opportunities are approved for Phase 1 participation to maintain control and quality.
