How the System Works

BIG SHARKS LTD operates a structured evaluation and onboarding process before any participation is approved.

1. Application & Screening

Individuals submit an application. Basic identity and intent checks are conducted to ensure suitability.

2. Evaluation Phase

Submitted applications are reviewed for clarity, risk alignment, and operational fit before approval.

3. Verification Process

Selected applicants may undergo additional verification depending on the program category.

4. Structured Onboarding

Approved participants are introduced to system guidelines, expectations, and operational structure.

5. Monitoring & Reporting

Activities are tracked and documented through internal reporting systems for transparency and accountability.

6. Participation Access

Final access is granted based on completion of onboarding and compliance requirements.

System Notice: BIG SHARKS LTD currently operates in a controlled rollout phase. Access is limited and subject to internal evaluation standards.
