Privacy Policy

Phase 1 transparency framework — this document explains how information is handled within the BIG SHARKS LTD system.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect basic identity and contact information such as name, phone number, location, and application details submitted through onboarding forms.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Information is collected strictly for evaluation, onboarding, verification, and structured participation within the BIG SHARKS LTD system.

3. Data Usage Policy

Collected information is used internally for decision-making, compliance screening, and system improvement. Data is not sold or transferred to third parties.

4. Data Protection

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are applied to protect participant information within the system.

5. User Rights

Participants may request access, correction, or removal of their submitted data subject to system requirements and compliance review.

6. System Status

BIG SHARKS LTD is currently operating in a controlled Phase 1 rollout. Policies may be updated as the system evolves.
